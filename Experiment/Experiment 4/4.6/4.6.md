# Self Join

## Question

The `student` table stores information about students, including their department and favorite course (`Course_id`). Use **SELF JOIN** to solve the following problems.

### Part 1: Students in the Same Department

Write an SQL query to find all pairs of students who belong to the **same department**. Display the `St_id`, `St_Name`, and `Department` for both students. Do not pair a student with themselves.

### Expected Output

| St_id | St_Name | Department | St_id | St_Name | Department |
|------:|-----------------|-------------|------:|-----------------|-------------|
| 1003 | David Lee | Mathematics | 1006 | Light Yagami | Mathematics |
| 1004 | Sarah Johnson | English | 1008 | Patrick Bateman | English |
| 1005 | Michael Chen | Biology | 1007 | Jordan | Biology |
| 1006 | Light Yagami | Mathematics | 1003 | David Lee | Mathematics |
| 1007 | Jordan | Biology | 1005 | Michael Chen | Biology |
| 1008 | Patrick Bateman | English | 1004 | Sarah Johnson | English |

---

### Part 2: Students with the Same Favorite Course

Write an SQL query to identify students who have selected the **same `Course_id`** as their favorite course. Display the `St_id`, `St_Name`, and `Course_id`. Order the result by `Course_id` in ascending order.

### Expected Output

| St_id | St_Name | Course_id |

| 1007 | Jordan | BIO103 |
